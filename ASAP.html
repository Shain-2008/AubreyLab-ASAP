Install AubreyLab ASAP App

Works full-screen on iPhone & Android

One-Tap App Install Ready

Add directly to your home screen & app drawer.

iPhone / iPad (Safari):

  1. Open your GitHub Pages link in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share icon (⎙ square with up arrow) at the bottom.
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen, then tap Add.

Android (Chrome):

  1. Open your GitHub Pages link in Chrome.
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu in the top-right corner.
  3. Tap Install app or Add to Home screen.

Once installed, AubreyLab ASAP opens in its own standalone app window without browser bars and remembers your selected journals & reading list on your phone.
